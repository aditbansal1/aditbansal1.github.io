## Abstract

A processor designed specifically for string processing. The work so far is background research, presented here as ongoing.

## Current investigation

Modularity and composability limits in digital logic primitives: why an N-bit primitive can’t be built by simply cascading N 1-bit instances without out-of-band state or glue logic. An adder needs its carry chain; a comparator needs its equality signal.

## Background

Research on the Quine-McCluskey algorithm for logic minimization.
