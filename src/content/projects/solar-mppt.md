## Abstract

Design and modeling of closed-loop control algorithms for solar energy capture, with all results obtained in simulation.

## Work

- Designed and modeled closed-loop control algorithms in MATLAB/Simulink, improving energy capture efficiency by 18% in simulation versus a baseline.
- Built dynamic simulations of Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT) across 50+ operating scenarios, evaluating stability and transient response under rapidly changing load conditions.
- Co-authored a technical review of microgrid control architectures and EV charging infrastructure topologies, analyzing grid load balance across energy-constrained regional networks.
